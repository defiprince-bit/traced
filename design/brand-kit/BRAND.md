# Traced brand kit
Traced, for TapeOut (OKX X Layer TapeOut hackathon, due 7 Dec 2026 23:59 UTC).
Tagline: "Every circuit, traced back to its source."
What it is: a read-only lookup. Paste a circuit ID and see who built it, its REF family tree, copies (byte-identical netlists with no REF back), and a free in-browser replay. Free replay leaves no on-chain trace, so usage isn't counted. Say that plainly.

## Logo
Mark A: a metaball node with four arms and a via dot in the centre hole. Always lume green on black, or mono white / black.
- x-avatar-400.png: X profile picture
- x-banner-1500x500.png/.svg: X header (the left area stays clear for the avatar overlap)
- lockup-*.svg/png: wordmark lockups; lockup-tapeout-* adds "FOR TAPEOUT"
- mark*.svg, favicon*: marks and favicons (small-16/32 are thickened cuts)
Don't recolour, stretch, outline or add effects. Keep clear space of at least the via-dot diameter around the mark.
